What is Spinning

Spinning is Lesotho's street-style car drifting culture. Skilled drivers spin their cars in circles while friends and fans cheer.

  • Watch live spin shows
  • Meet drivers and see custom cars
  • Enjoy food, music and mountain views
Safety-tip: Stay behind the barriers and follow the marshals.
